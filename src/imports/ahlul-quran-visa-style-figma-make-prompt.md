# Figma Make Prompt — International Qur'an Conference Landing Page (Travel-Booking Style)

Paste the prompt below into Figma Make in one go.

---

Design a landing page for the "5th International Qur'an Conference," organized by the Ahlul Qur'an Foundation, taking place Sunday, 4th October 2026 at the International Conference Centre, University of Ibadan, Nigeria. This year's theme is "The Qur'an and the Reformation of the Ummah." The layout should follow a modern travel-booking/discovery website style — a huge full-bleed hero photo with an oversized wordmark, a dark filter bar overlapping the hero's bottom edge, and card-based "browse and pick" sections — adapted here to sell conference tickets and showcase the event's speaker lineup instead of travel destinations.

## Style

- Background: warm off-white/cream (#F7F6EF)
- Primary dark colour (nav overlay text, filter bars, footer): deep near-black green (#16201A), with an even deeper green (#0A331C) for the hero overlay and CTA banner
- Core brand green (accents, tags): forest green (#12522F)
- Accent/highlight colour (CTA buttons, key headline words, badges): warm gold (#FFC153)
- Secondary accent (urgency, ticket CTAs): brick red (#B5352A)
- Headline font: a bold serif with real weight (e.g. Merriweather) used at a huge scale for the hero wordmark and section headlines — the hero headline especially should be enormous, filling most of the hero width
- Body font: a clean modern sans-serif (e.g. Manrope)
- Generously rounded corners (14–20px) on cards, filter bars, and photos — soft and modern, not sharp-edged
- Small rounded pill badges throughout (e.g. "✦ We are Ahlul Qur'an Foundation," "★ 250+ Tickets Secured") sitting on plain background between sections
- Avoid: stock travel-brochure clichés, sharp corners, dense paragraph text — keep copy short and confident, like a premium event platform

## Layout — top to bottom

**1. Hero (full-bleed photo with overlay nav)** — a large full-height background photo (a conference hall or gathering scene) with a dark gradient overlay (darker at top and bottom, lighter in the middle) so both the nav and hero text stay readable. Overlaid nav bar: a small circular gold logo mark + "Ahlul Qur'an Foundation" wordmark in white on the left, centered nav links (About / Sessions / Speakers / Contact) in white, and a small rounded "🔍 Search sessions" pill on the right. Collapses to a hamburger icon on mobile. Below the nav, vertically centered in the hero, an enormous bold serif headline reading simply "Qur'an" (filling most of the hero's width, similar in scale to a single-word brand headline), with a short white subtext line below it about the conference theme and one-day format.

**2. Overlapping filter bar** — a dark rounded rectangular bar that overlaps the bottom edge of the hero photo (extending below it, so roughly half sits on the photo and half below it), divided into 4 label/value columns in white text — "Event" (5th Int'l Qur'an Conference), "Venue" (Int'l Conference Centre, UI), "Date" (Sun, 4th October 2026), "Ticket From" (₦3,000) — followed by a solid gold "Get Ticket →" button filling the final column.

**3. Trust badges row** — directly below the filter bar, a row with two small pill badges on the left ("✦ We are Ahlul Qur'an Foundation," "★ 250+ Tickets Secured") with generous whitespace around them.

**4. Big mission headline** — centered, large, lighter-weight serif text: "The Qur'an guides to what is upright." with the phrase "what is upright" in gold.

**5. "Not just a conference" value section** — two-column. Left: a small green rounded tag pill ("01 · This Year's Theme"), a bold headline ("Not just a conference — a gathering."), a short paragraph about scholars, panelists, and reciters coming together for a day of recitation and dialogue, and a solid dark rounded pill button ("Get a Ticket"). Right: an asymmetric photo collage — one large rounded photo on the left labeled "Main Conference Hall" with a caption underneath, and two smaller stacked rounded photos on the right, each with a small dark label tag overlapping the bottom-left corner ("Panel Discussion," "Guest Reciters").

**6. "Pick the Session" section** — a header row with a small green tag pill ("Program Lineup 2026") above a large headline ("Pick the Session"), and a short right-aligned description line about the day's scholars, panelists, and reciters. Below that, a white rounded filter bar with 4 columns (Track, Speaker, Time, Ticket price range) plus a solid dark "Discover" button in the final column. Below the filter bar, a row of 3 session cards, each containing: a small header row with a session title and speaker name on the left and a small gold "location" tag on the right (e.g. "Main Hall," "Panel Room"), a rounded photo placeholder with a small dark "session type" tag overlapping its top-right corner (Lecture / Panel / Recitation), a meta row listing additional speaker names, and a footer row (separated by a thin divider) showing the session category and date.

**7. Footer** — solid dark near-black background. Three-column layout: (1) logo repeated in the serif font ("Ahlul Qur'an Foundation," with "Qur'an" in gold) plus a short tagline, (2) Quick Links (About / Sessions / Get Tickets), (3) Contact (phone number, email, Instagram handle, TikTok handle). A bottom bar below a thin divider with copyright text and a small secondary note.

## Requirements

- Fully responsive down to mobile: the overlapping filter bar and the "Pick the Session" filter bar both collapse to a single stacked column, the photo collage becomes a simple stacked layout, session cards stack vertically, nav collapses to a hamburger icon.
- Keep the "browse and filter" visual language (filter bars, badge tags, card grids) even though there is only one event — the filter fields can display static/example values (as described above) rather than needing to be functional, since this is a single-day, single-event landing page rather than a true multi-listing platform.
- Use real event details wherever factual: date (Sunday, 4th October 2026), venue (International Conference Centre, University of Ibadan), theme, ticket pricing (₦3,000 early bird / ₦5,000 regular), the "250+ tickets secured" figure, and all named scholars, panelists, and reciters from the program lineup, rather than placeholder text for anything factual.
- Photography should be placeholder blocks for now, in warm earthy and deep green tones consistent with the palette above.
